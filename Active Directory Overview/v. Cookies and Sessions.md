# Cookies and Sessions

## Cookies

Store information on the client side.

Examples:

- PHPSESSID
- JSESSIONID

---

## Session Cookies

Removed after browser closes.

---

## Persistent Cookies

Remain after browser restart.

---

## Cookie Security Flags

### Secure

HTTPS only.

### HttpOnly

Blocks JavaScript access.

### SameSite

Helps prevent CSRF.

---

## Risks

- Session Hijacking
- Session Fixation
